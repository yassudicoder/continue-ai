WPT 05FIELD MANUAL

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED PLAINLY.

Q1Is it free?+

Yes — completely free, no account, no paywall. Every shipped feature is unlocked.

Q2Do you read or store my conversations?+

No. Capture and packaging happen entirely on your device. There are no servers of ours, nothing is transmitted to us, and there's no account or telemetry. A conversation only goes to another AI when you transfer it — and then it goes to that AI, not to us.

Q3Which AI tools does it work with?+

Capture on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Continue on any of eight — those three plus DeepSeek, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, and Google AI Studio.

Q4Where is my data, and what if I uninstall?+

Everything stays in your browser's local storage on your own machine. Export any conversation as PDF, HTML, an image, Markdown, or JSON whenever you like. Uninstalling removes the extension's local data.

Q5Does it work on really long chats?+

Yes. Capture grabs the full thread — including long, virtualized conversations where the page only keeps part of the chat mounted — so messages aren't silently dropped.
